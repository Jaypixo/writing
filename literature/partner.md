title: "How I fell in love with my partner"
date: 2026-06-02
category: "Personal"

Okay, so, here goes nothing. I’ve been trying to write this down for a while but every time I open my notes app I just kinda stare at the blinking cursor. But I think I’m ready now. Or at least, ready enough.

So like, back when I was still cool with *that dude* (you know the one, we don’t say his name anymore lmao), there was this girl. But yeah *her*. At first she was just "oh that’s the girl who’s obsessed with him" to me. She kinda snaked her way into our hangouts, always finding a reason to show up, laughing too loud at his stupid jokes. I thought she was really annoying at times. Like, not even in a mean way just in a “why are you here this is weird” way. But honestly I did think she was kinda hot to be bruttally honsted. Never said a word about it for obvious reasons.

I didn’t like her. I didn’t *dislike* her exactly but I definitely didn’t get the hype. She was just... there. And then everything with them blew up (dramatically, obviously) and I stopped talking to both of them for different reasons. Her, I just kinda forgot about. She wasn't my problem.

Months go by. I’m bored, she’s bored, she snaps me out of nowhere. And I don’t know why but I replied. Probably because I had nothing better to do. And then we just kept talking. At first it was dry. “wyd” “nm u” the usual. But then it got genuinely funny. She's actually really witty when she's not trying to impress someone.

Then came the casual flirting. And then the *less casual* flirting if you know what I mean. It was never serious though. I told myself it wasn't serious. Because, well, she had a reputation. A hoe reputation, at least in my head. I mean after what happened with that dude? Yeah I wasn't about to catch actual feelings for someone like that. That would be stupid.

So we'd call. A lot. Mostly late at night when neither of us could sleep. It was fun and easy. I liked talking to her but I kept her at an arm's length emotionally basically. She was an option in the back of my mind but not a real one. More like a "maybe if I get desperate" kind of thought. Which sounds bad now but I'm being honest.

Then one night. And I remember this so clearly it's stupid.

We were just on the phone. I think I was playing some game and she was doing homework or something. Just existing together. And then she got a text. She goes quiet for a second, and then she just starts crying. Like not even pretty crying. Full on sobbing where you can't breathe. And I'm asking "what what happened" and she tells me. It's a text from one of her friends. A girl I don't even know. And the text read a lot like a suicide note.

At this point she's completely losing it. Because she already has her own stuff going on, mental health wise, and now her friend might be doing something unalive. And I'm lying there on the phone feeling so useless. stayed on that call for hours. I talked her through breathing. I told her to call the cops, to call someone idk, to not blame herself. This call kept going for hours.

And somewhere in the middle of all that, it hit me. Like a truck honestly.

I saw her. Not the annoying girl from before. Not the "hoe" I had labeled her as. Not the option. Just her. A person. A real, messy, scared, caring person who was hurting so bad for someone she loved. And I realized I *cared*. Like actually, genuinely cared. In a "if something happened to her I would lose my mind" way.

That's when I caught feelings. For real. Not the weird kind.

After that night, everything eventually changed. We started actually talking. Like deep talking. About her childhood, about my fears, about why she acted the way she did back then (spoiler: sexualizing herself is the only way she felt loved by a lot of dudes). The flirting became softer. The sexting stopped because it felt too shallow all of a sudden.

It's funny. I spent so long judging her based on one situation with one dude. And now? Now she's literally the person I trust the most. And I almost missed it because I was too busy being an idiot lol.

Anyway yeah. That's the story. Not a movie or whatever but it's ours. And I'm kinda glad I didn't have the right words for it until now. Because now I know exactly how it felt. It felt like realizing you were wrong about someone. And being so, so happy about it.

I used to dislike her and now she's the most important person in my life all of the sudden.
