---
title: "The Quiet Library"
date: 2026-06-01
category: "Literary Fiction"
---

The library was quiet in the way old houses are quiet, as if every shelf and beam
were holding a memory in place.

I found the note tucked between two books with cracked spines. It had no signature,
only a sentence written in a careful hand:

> Come back when the light changes.

So I did.

Each evening, just before sunset, the windows turned gold and the dust in the air
looked briefly alive. That was when the stories seemed to wake.
