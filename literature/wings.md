---
title: "Wings"
date: 2026-05-27
category: "Poetry"
---

The demon claims to be an angel
Her wings are black and red
Fleshy and leathery

But she paints them white
And stitches on feathers
And they call her an angel
They say she's an angel

She worships like a saint
And dresses like a bride
But she knows she's a demon

Though she has wings
She believes she's a demon
Even fallen angels
Have their wings
